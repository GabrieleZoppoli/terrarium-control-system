*Drosera regia*, the king sundew, is the giant of the genus, with leaves reaching 60 cm in mature plants. It is endemic to a small area of the Bain's Kloof valley in the Western Cape of South Africa and tolerates Mediterranean-type conditions, but its requirements do not fit the highland tropical regime.

## Why a separate setup

*D. regia* does best with:
- Cool nights (8–15 °C) and moderately warm days (20–28 °C), a Mediterranean rather than tropical regime
- Strong, direct light
- A pronounced winter dormancy, which the highland terrarium does not provide
- Mineral-poor, peat-based substrate kept drier than for most *Drosera*
