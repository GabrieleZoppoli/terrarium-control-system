A radial phylogenetic dendrogram of the whole collection, clade → order → family → genus → species. Each leaf carries source, status and, where photographed, a slideshow that opens on hover. The tree opens at genus level, one tip per genus with its species count; click a genus to fan out its species and click it again to fold them back. Search jumps straight to a plant, and *All species* shows the full tree. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Esc to reset.

Built with [D3.js](https://d3js.org/). The dataset is generated from the master Excel inventory; every photograph is watermarked CC BY-SA 4.0.
