## Overview

Highland *Nepenthes* are tropical pitcher plants from the upper montane forests of Borneo, Sumatra, the Philippines, and Sulawesi, typically 1,500--3,000 m elevation. Unlike their lowland relatives (which require sustained heat), highland species thrive in the same cool, humid conditions as tepui and Andean cloud forest plants.

## Position in Terrarium

**Lower to middle zone**: moderate shade and the coolest air, since cold air sinks from the evaporator above. The climbing habit takes them upward while the roots stay in the cooler zone.

## Substrate

Kanuma and long-fibre sphagnum. No saucers or trays: at 80–95 % humidity the roots need no extra water.

## Cultivation notes

The collection emphasises **Sumatran endemics** (*N. aristolochioides* clone NM03, *N. inermis*, *N. tenuis* 'Reddish Leaves', *N. jamban*) from the Barisan Mountains at 1,800–2,500 m, alongside **Philippine endemics** (*N. argentii* from Sibuyan, *N. micramphora* from Mount Hamiguitan, Mindanao) from comparable elevations. Nine alive, one given to a friend whose setup suited it better, no losses to date.

Plant by plant:

- **N. aristolochioides (NM03)** arrived at Ø 5–8 cm and has kept an even pitcher-to-leaf ratio. The domed pitchers started forming from the third or fourth leaf after acclimation.
- **N. pitopangii 'Ivory Colored Form' Clone 01** was a rooted cutting; it was slower to establish than the seed-grown Sumatrans but is now producing typical white pitchers.
- One accession is a **"Fake Pitopangii"**, sold as *N. pitopangii* (Ivory Colored Form); the pitcher shape says otherwise. It stays in the collection under that label.
- **N. jamban** (the squat "toilet-pitcher" endemic) is the one plant in the group that dislikes the evaporator side of the cabinet; it pitchers properly only in the shadier, less draughty corner.
- **N. glabrata**, the Sulawesi endemic, came from a growlist with no recorded acquisition date. It has been the easiest of the lot and holds its fluted pitchers for months.

The highland regime (night minima around 13–14 °C) suits all of the above. Summer heat has never needed intervention: the cabinet is thermally insulated from the room.
