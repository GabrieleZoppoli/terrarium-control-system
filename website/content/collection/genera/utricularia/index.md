## Overview

Bladderworts. The epiphytic section *Orchidioides* (tepui cliff faces and Neotropical cloud-forest canopies) lives in the highland terrarium as kokedama; terrestrial species live elsewhere. Three of four sect. *Orchidioides* species were lost, the largest single cultivation failure in the collection. The hybrid *U.* × *nelumbifolia* × *reniformis* survived; *U. quelchii*, re-acquired in 2023, flowered in 2026.
