The collection logs every plant I have acquired since 2016: 400+ accessions in more than 90 genera. About 300 are alive today; the rest were lost to cultivation mistakes or transport, or were given away. Overall survival is around 74 %, close to 100 % in *Heliamphora*, highland *Nepenthes* and *Dracula*.
